# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project community.

Be constructive, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect privacy, and avoid discriminatory or abusive behavior. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the contact channel configured on the hosting platform.
